separately capped and finite. Their unit fragments, invocation identities,

A separate one-shot manager timer is armed BEFORE workload allocation. Its

preallocated aggregate cgroup generation through a retained directory FD.

removes no containers, volumes or data and never treats killed processes as

native qualification; source inspection is not substituted for that evidence.

JSON. The daemon's actual executable, birth, command line, config hash, socket

## Settlement, recovery and data preservation

WAF startup-task completion/failure is not entrypoint quiescence. The V6 sticky
startup guards are unchanged. Only the external SDK unit's actual invocation
exit and empty cgroup permit backend recovery. Proxy readers are joined by
manager quiescence first. The recovery path checks full IDs, Created, immutable
image IDs, ownership labels, mounts and generation; gracefully stops, removes
without volume removal, and requires actual 404 plus final empty inventory.
Each independent failure is retained. A later successful cleanup does not turn
the original qualification failure into a pass. A stopped daemon without a
retained final absence proof remains failed/quarantined, never inferred empty.

V2 also fences the allocating coordinator itself before independent recovery.
The launcher persists its unpredictable transient-unit description, exact
executable/arguments/source and cgroup before dispatch. The coordinator retains
its actual InvocationID and process birth/executable/cgroup before allocating
anything. Recovery reobserves that exact fence, gracefully stops that unit and
requires terminal invocation timestamps, MainPID zero and the exact cgroup empty
BEFORE opening the coordinator's resource ledger. Missing or ambiguous dispatch
is retained, not interpreted as absence; a launcher timeout is not quiescence.

V3 gives coordinator and recovery distinct unpredictable temporary receipt paths.
Recovery makes no receipt write before the verified exact coordinator stop and
empty-cgroup barrier, so another interrupted writer cannot prevent that stop.
Only then does it attempt three bounded writes of its own receipt. Write faults
and interrupted writer artifacts are retained; foreign temporary files are never
adopted or removed. Exhausted persistence still fences backend recovery and does
not claim cleanup acceptance, even though the coordinator has been stopped.

Anonymous image-created volume data and private daemon roots are preserved,
not deleted by cleanup. They live only on the disposable hosted runner until
its platform lease ends. Receipts distinguish active-resource quiescence from
retained data; no persistent external/user volume is admitted or removed.
